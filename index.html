About Us

RS Pixels started in 2021 as a local creative and printing business in San Roque, San Leonardo, Nueva Ecija. We specialize in apparel printing and advertising materials, combining creative design with practical printing solutions. From custom shirts to tarpaulins, stickers, and large-format prints, we help customers turn their ideas into finished products.

Mission

To provide quality printing and apparel services with reliable customer service, creative solutions, and attention to detail while making every project convenient and worthwhile for our customers.

Vision

To grow as a trusted local printing and apparel business known for quality products, creative work, dependable service, and strong relationships with our customers and community.

Our Services

RS Pixels provides practical printing and apparel services for personal projects, schools, organizations, events, and businesses. Our services include custom apparel printing, tarpaulins, stickers, signage, and large-format printing based on your design and project needs.

Our Products

Choose from custom T-shirts and apparel, tarpaulins, stickers, signage, and other printed materials. Our products can be prepared for events, uniforms, promotions, business branding, school activities, and personal projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and products.

Where is the main store located at?

Our main store is located at Looban St., San Roque, San Leonardo, Nueva Ecija.

What are the available payment methods?

Customers may contact RS Pixels to confirm the available payment methods for their order before making a payment.

How fast is the typical shipping time after ordering products?

Shipping time depends on the product, order quantity, location, and delivery service. RS Pixels can provide an estimated delivery time when the order details are confirmed.

Are there return policies when the product I received is unsatisfactory?

If the product received is unsatisfactory, please contact RS Pixels and provide the order details and relevant photos so the concern can be reviewed.

Can I cancel an order before it ships out?

Customers may request cancellation before an order is shipped. For orders already in transit, please contact RS Pixels as soon as possible so the concern can be checked.
